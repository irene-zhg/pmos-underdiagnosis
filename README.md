## What is PMOS?
Polyendocrine Metabolic Ovarian Syndrome (PMOS), previously known as Polycystic Ovarian Syndrome (PCOS), is the most common endocrine disorder in reproductive-age women. PMOS is a complex disorder that affects reproductive, metabolic, dermatological, and psychological health. Despite its severity, no cure exists, but early diagnosis and intervention is important to mitigate long-term risks like type-2 diabetes, insulin resistance, cardiovascular disease and infertility.
